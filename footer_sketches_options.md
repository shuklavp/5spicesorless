# 5 Spices or Less: Thematic White Pencil Sketch Illustrations for Footer Banners

To replace the repetitive mortar and pestle photograph across all pages, seven unique architectural white pencil sketches have been created, directly reflecting the theme of each page.

All illustrations are rendered in crisp, luminous white pencil linework with fine cross-hatching on pitch-black backgrounds, blending naturally into each page's dark palette.

---

### Page-by-Page Options & Selected Illustrations

#### 1. Home Broadside (`/`): *The Five Spices Manifesto*
* **Selected Illustration (Option A):**  
  **Traditional Brass Spice Box (Masala Dabba)** (`/footer-spice-box.jpg`)  
  *Visual:* An open traditional round Indian spice container with compartments containing whole star anise, cinnamon sticks, cardamom pods, cloves, and cumin seeds with an ornate lid and brass spoon.
* **Alternative Options:**
  * *Option B:* Hand-carved stone mortar and pestle with scattered cardamom and cinnamon.
  * *Option C:* Minimalist iron skillet simmering whole spices over quiet embers.
